How to Use

After cleansing and toning, dispense 1 pump onto the palm of your hands before patting them onto your face. Add a couple more pump if intending to apply across the neck and chest area. Finish with a moisturizer, ideally alongside SPF 30 or greater when used during the day.

For new retinoid users: use two days on, two days off. Work your way up to every other day, then to daily, evening use. Follow with a gentle oil or moisturiser.

For advanced retinoid users: use daily, in the evenings. Follow with a gentle oil or moisturiser.

For sensitive skin: Apply a few drops of a gentle, moisturising oil to your skin before applying the serum.

Cautions

Do not use on irritated or sensitive skin. If irritation, rash, or patchy dry skin develops at or around the site of application during use, especially after prolonged or repeated use, stop use immediately and consult a doctor. Store in a cool place.
* Due to the Retinoid content, this formula is not recommended if pregnant/breastfeeding.
